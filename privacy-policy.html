Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Information

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you visit our website, contact us, or inquire about drainage services in Los Angeles and surrounding areas.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact forms, phone calls, emails, or other communications. This may include your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, property location, and details about your drainage needs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to respond to inquiries, understand your requested drainage service, coordinate communication, provide relevant service information, and improve the overall website experience.

3. Contractor Network Communication

When appropriate for your inquiry, information may be shared with contractors or subcontractors within our network who work with us in connection with drainage services. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to address your service request or related communication.

4. Website Usage Information

Our website may automatically collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, referring pages, and website activity. This information may be used to understand website performance, improve functionality, and maintain website security.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support website functionality and understand how visitors interact with our pages. Depending on the tools enabled on this website, analytics services may also collect information about visits and website usage.

6. How We Protect Information

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to help protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may use third-party services or contain links to external websites. These third parties operate under their own privacy practices, and we encourage visitors to review their policies before providing personal information.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have submitted through this website or to request that certain information be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience seeking drainage service information and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in website practices, services, technology, or applicable requirements. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Questions About This Privacy Policy?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through our website is handled, please contact us for assistance.
