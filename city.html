Property-Focused Guidance

Periods of intense rain can increase runoff and expose poor drainage, low spots, or overwhelmed drains around Hollywood homes and properties.

Yard flooding may result from low areas, poor grading, compacted soil, or inadequate drainage that cannot move stormwater away efficiently.

A French drain may help with recurring saturated soil or groundwater concerns, but the right solution depends on your property's drainage conditions.

Hillside drainage may involve grading, collection drains, catch basins, or controlled discharge routes based on the property's slope and runoff patterns.

The right system may include French drains, catch basins, trench drains, or grading depending on where water collects and how runoff moves.
