Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment to Website Accessibility

We are committed to providing an accessible online experience for people seeking drainage service information in Los Angeles and surrounding areas. We continually work to make our website easier to navigate, understand, and use.

1. Our Accessibility Commitment

We aim to provide website content and functionality that can be accessed by as many visitors as possible, including people who use assistive technologies or alternative methods to browse and interact with websites.

2. Accessibility Standards

Our accessibility efforts are guided by generally recognized web accessibility practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). We work to improve usability, readability, navigation, and access to important website information.

3. Website Navigation

We strive to maintain clear menus, descriptive page headings, meaningful links, and a consistent website structure so visitors can locate information about drainage services, service areas, contact options, and other important resources.

4. Images and Visual Content

Where appropriate, meaningful website images include descriptive alternative text to help visitors using screen readers understand relevant visual content. Decorative images may be handled differently when they do not provide essential information.

5. Keyboard Accessibility

We work to support navigation and interaction with important website features using a keyboard wherever reasonably possible, including menus, links, buttons, forms, and other interactive elements.

6. Forms and Contact Information

We aim to make contact forms and communication options clear and understandable. Visitors who experience difficulty submitting a form can contact us directly by phone or email for assistance with drainage service inquiries.

7. Third-Party Content

Some pages may include content, maps, tools, or functionality provided by third parties. While we strive to work with accessible resources, certain third-party features may operate outside our direct control.

8. Ongoing Accessibility Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing effort. As our website, content, and technology evolve, we may review and improve features to provide a more convenient and accessible experience for visitors.

9. Accessibility Assistance

If you encounter an accessibility barrier while using this website, please let us know. Providing details about the page, feature, or content involved can help us understand the issue and work toward an appropriate solution.

Need Accessibility Assistance?

If you have difficulty accessing information on this website or need assistance with a drainage service inquiry, please contact us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or assistance you need.
