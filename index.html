Drainage FAQs

Answers to common drainage questions

Understand common drainage problems, what causes water to collect, and which factors influence the right solution for your property.

Standing water can result from low areas, compacted soil, poor grading, restricted runoff, or inadequate drainage around the property.

The right system depends on where water originates, how it moves, property slope, soil conditions, and where runoff can discharge safely.

Proper drainage can help redirect surface water away from vulnerable areas when the source and flow path are correctly identified.

Cost varies with the drainage problem, system type, property access, excavation, materials, site conditions, and the overall scope of work.
