Where Channel & Trench Drains Work Best

Linear drains are particularly effective where runoff moves across paved surfaces rather than collecting at one point. Common applications include sloped driveways, garage entrances, patios, courtyards, walkways, and other hardscape areas exposed to concentrated rainwater.

Proper installation requires the drain to work with existing surface elevations. Licensed contractors within our network consider channel position, grate type, drainage capacity, pipe routing, and outlet conditions when planning the system.
